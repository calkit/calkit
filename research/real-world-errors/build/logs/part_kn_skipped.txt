Kapoor & Narayanan slice: fields and sources skipped
Frame: the 41-row table at https://reproducible.cs.princeton.edu/ (30 distinct
fields). Rule: per field, sources in table order; take the first paper the
source names as affected; fall through to the field's next source if none.
The political science row (K&N's own civil war case study) contributes all 4
papers they found leakage in.

Fields with no case taken:
- Nutrition research (Ivanescu et al. 2016): methods overview; no paper named.
- Software engineering (Tu et al. 2018): ACM full text not accessible; no
  affected paper identifiable.
- Clinical epidemiology (Christodoulou et al. 2019): systematic review
  reporting aggregate validation bias (48/71 studies); no paper named in
  accessible text.
- Tractography (Poulin et al. 2019): survey; abstract names no paper; full
  text paywalled.
- Molecular biology (Park & Marcotte 2012; Urban et al. 2020; Li et al. 2022):
  first two describe the problem generally or test anonymized methods (named
  only in supplementary tables); Li 2022 paywalled.
- Neuropsychiatry (Poldrack et al. 2020; Shim et al. 2021): Poldrack is an
  aggregate review; Shim demonstrates on simulated and own EEG data. No paper
  named in accessible text (Shim full text not read in full).
- IT operations (Lyu et al. 2021): empirical study of splitting choices; no
  affected paper identified (full text not accessible).
- Radiology (Roberts et al. 2021; Gidwani et al. 2023): Nature MI and RSNA
  pages blocked; secondary summaries describe "Frankenstein" datasets and
  pediatric controls without naming a paper.
- Genomics (Barnett et al. 2022): medRxiv blocked; meta-regression of
  aggregate study features.
- Genetics (Whalen et al. 2022): review; abstract names no paper; paywalled.
- Sustainable energy (Geslin et al. 2023): perspective reanalyzing the
  Severson battery dataset; shows charging-data features leak protocol
  information but doesn't single out a published model as flawed, so the
  affected paper is ambiguous.
- Ecology (Stock et al. 2023): Nature E&E and Zenodo copies blocked; lab
  summary names no paper.
- Mining (Munagala et al. 2024): survey; not checked in depth, no paper
  identified.

Field where the first source named no paper and a later source was used:
- Medicine: Bouwmeester et al. 2012 names no paper -> Chiavegatto Filho et
  al. 2021 (KN-2). Vandewiele et al. 2021 (medicine, later row) names Fergus
  et al. 2013 PLoS One (SMOTE before splitting; reported AUC 0.92 reproduced
  only with the flaw) but was not taken under the one-per-field rule.
- Neuroimaging: Whelan & Garavan 2014 names no paper -> Ahmed et al. 2022
  TPAMI (KN-3).
