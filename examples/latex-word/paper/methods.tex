\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

We measured the velocity field with a two-component laser Doppler
velocimeter~\citep{lee2018}.
The sampling frequency was $f_s = 1$~kHz and the
integral time scale was estimated from the autocorrelation.

The mean velocity deficit is modeled as
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\Delta U}{U_\infty} = \frac{C_T}{8 (1 + k x/D)^2},
  \label{eq:wake}
\end{equation}
where $C_T$ is the thrust coefficient and $k$ is the wake growth rate.
Figure~\ref{fig:one} shows the result, and Table~\ref{tab:params} lists
the parameters.

\subsection{Wake model}
\label{sec:model}

Far downstream, the momentum the rotor extracts is carried by the
deficit, so that
\begin{equation}
  \int_{A} \rho\, U \left( \Uinf - U \right) \mathrm{d}A
  = \frac{1}{2} \rho\, C_T A_0 \Uinf^2,
  \label{eq:momentum}
\end{equation}
where $A_0 = \pi D^2/4$ is the rotor area.
Assuming a self-similar Gaussian profile~\citep{bastankhah2014} and
satisfying Eq.~\eqref{eq:momentum} gives
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\Delta U}{\Uinf} = \left( 1 - \sqrt{1 - \frac{C_T}{8 (\sigma/D)^2}}
  \right) \exp\left[ -\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{r}{\sigma} \right)^2
  \right],
  \label{eq:gaussian}
\end{equation}
where the wake widens as $\sigma/D = k^* x/D + \varepsilon$, with
$\varepsilon = 0.2\sqrt{\beta}$ and
$\beta = \frac{1}{2} \left( 1 + \sqrt{1 - C_T} \right) / \sqrt{1 - C_T}$.
The rotor adds turbulence to the wake~\citep{crespo1996}:
\begin{align}
  I_+ &= 0.73\, a^{0.8325} I_\infty^{0.0325}
    \left( \frac{x}{D} \right)^{-0.32}, \label{eq:added} \\
  a &= \frac{1}{2} \left( 1 - \sqrt{1 - C_T} \right),
\end{align}
and the growth rate follows from the local turbulence intensity
$I = \sqrt{I_\infty^2 + I_+^2}$ as
\begin{equation*}
  k^* = \begin{cases}
    0.38\, I + 0.004, & I < 0.3, \\
    0.12, & \text{otherwise}.
  \end{cases}
\end{equation*}
Momentum is transported into the wake by the Reynolds shear stress
$-\rho\,\overline{u'v'}$, which balances the streamwise decay
$\partial U / \partial x$ of the deficit.
For a farm of $N$ turbines, deficits combine in quadrature, so turbine
$i$ produces
\begin{equation}
  P_i = \frac{1}{2} \rho A_0 C_P \left( \Uinf
  - \sqrt{\sum_{j \neq i} \Delta U_{ij}^2} \right)^3,
  \qquad P = \sum_{i=1}^{N} P_i .
  \label{eq:farm}
\end{equation}
