# Computerized cognitive training and cognitive scores in older adults

## Methods

Older adults were recruited at four community centers and randomized to a
12-week computerized cognitive training program or a waitlist control.
Eligible participants were aged 60–85. A cognitive composite score was
assessed at baseline and at weeks 4, 8, and 12; incomplete assessments were
not scored. The analysis sample comprises participants with a scored
baseline and week-12 assessment ({astra}`outputs.analysis_data`).

The primary analysis was an analysis of covariance (ANCOVA): an ordinary
least squares model of week-12 score on arm, baseline score, age (per 10
years), and education (secondary, college, or graduate, with secondary as
the reference category). As a sensitivity analysis, we modeled the change in
score from baseline to week 12 on arm, age, and education
({astra}`decisions.adjustment`; universes `baseline` and `change_score`).
