# Testimonials

## Rebecca (Becca) McCabe {#becca-mccabe}

> In the first conference paper I wrote back in 2022,
> I had a scare where my process of manually screenshotting MATLAB figures
> and uploading them to Overleaf
> almost led me to publish inconsistent results
> when I forgot to rerun some intermediate steps.
> To ensure the integrity of my research,
> I resolved to better automate my workflow,
> and spent too much time creating some clunky one-off reproducibility code
> that only partially addressed my needs.
> Then in 2025 I stumbled upon Calkit.
> With its structured approach, cross-language capabilities,
> and use of software engineering best practices,
> Calkit was more comprehensive, flexible, and future-proof
> than my hacky custom script, and
> I relied on it heavily for the remainder of my projects.
> Using Calkit significantly relieved my anxiety
> about inadvertently contributing to the replication crisis;
> streamlined review, collaboration, and reproduction workflows
> that would have been otherwise infeasible;
> and improved the usability and visibility of my work for other researchers.
> Importantly, Calkit scales to more complex projects,
> and I can't imagine how I would have
> kept the 87 figures and 92 numerical results in my dissertation straight
> without this tool.
> I also received impressive technical support,
> including fast and helpful responses to my questions, bug reports,
> and feature requests.
> I would recommend Calkit to software-savvy researchers
> (and even tech-traditionalist collaborators)
> who care about the accuracy and transparency of their results.

—[Rebecca (Becca) McCabe](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r3eP1gIAAAAJ&hl=en),
PhD, Cornell (2026)
