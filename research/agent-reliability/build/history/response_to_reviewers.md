# Response to reviewers

Manuscript JGER-2026-0412, "Computerized cognitive training and cognitive
scores in older adults"

We thank the reviewers for their careful reading. Their comments are in
italics, and our responses follow.

## Reviewer 1

*1.1. The trial is well designed and the report is clear. The authors
should state how incomplete assessments were handled.*

Incomplete assessments were not scored, and the analysis sample comprises
participants with a scored baseline and week-12 assessment. The Methods now
say so.

*1.2. Figure 1 shows standard errors, which understate the uncertainty
readers care about. Please show confidence intervals.*

Figure 1's error bars now show 95% confidence intervals.

## Reviewer 2

*2.1. The Methods give the eligible ages as 60–80, but the registered
protocol gives 60–85. Why were participants aged 81–85 excluded?*

Thank you for catching this. Our analysis used the age range from an early
draft of the protocol. The registered protocol, and the enrollment database,
give the eligible ages as 60–85, so the analysis now includes all eligible
participants aged 60–85, and the Methods and results are updated.

*2.2. Please report the change-score analysis as a sensitivity analysis
rather than a second primary analysis.*

It is now described as a sensitivity analysis.
