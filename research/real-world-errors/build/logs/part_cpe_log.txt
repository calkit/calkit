CPE stratum log (computational physics and engineering errata)

Journals chosen up front, with ISSN used in the Crossref issn filter:
 Physical Review Fluids 2469-990X; Physical Review E 2470-0045; Journal of Fluid Mechanics 0022-1120; AIAA Journal 0001-1452; Journal of Computational Physics 0021-9991; Geoscientific Model Development 1991-9603 (1991-959X returned nothing); Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems 1942-2466; Journal of Climate 0894-8755; Applied Energy 0306-2619; Computers & Structures 0045-7949.

Frame query: api.crossref.org/works?filter=issn:<ISSN>,from-pub-date:2018-01-01,until-pub-date:2025-12-31&query.bibliographic=<term>, for term in erratum, corrigendum, correction; 200 rows per page with cursor paging, stopping at the first page with no title matches (relevance-sorted). Titles kept if they start with Erratum/Corrigendum/Correction (to|:) or end with '- CORRIGENDUM'/'- ERRATUM' (JFM style). Titles like 'Correction of ...' (research papers) dropped. Generic User-Agent, 1 s sleeps.
 Exception: GMD has no separate corrigenda after 2017 in Crossref (Copernicus now corrects in place), so its window was extended to 2008-2025; all 6 found are 2010-2014.
 JAMES: the query returned no notices (Wiley errata not matched); logged as empty.
 Limitation: relevance-based query may miss notices whose titles lack the terms.

Access: notice text read by curl+HTML parsing (Cambridge JFM pages, AMS J. Climate pages), Copernicus PDFs via pdftotext (GMD), and OpenAlex abstract_inverted_index for APS, AIAA, and Elsevier (their sites return 403 to automated fetches). Notices with <25 words of text were counted unreadable.

Sampling: I screened every readable notice (a census of the readable frame, not a sample), since screening was cheap once texts were fetched. All kept cases are coded in part_cpe.csv. To honor the 20-40 target, the selection column marks the first 40 kept cases in a seeded order (random.seed(20261127); random.shuffle of the sorted union of readable DOIs) as cpe_errata_census_seed20261127_first40; the rest are ..._overflow. Filter on that if you want exactly 40.

Screening rule: keep notices citing a code bug, numerical or computational error, a processing/input error (units, calibration, conversion, wrong file), a methods-vs-code mismatch, or wrong values/figures relative to computed outputs (including typos in reported values, so stale vs typo can be compared). Screen out: equation typos or analytic derivation errors not propagated into computed results; author, affiliation, funding, citation, style, and publisher production errors; pure label/notation errors.

Staleness coding: stale_result only when the notice says the figure/value came from an earlier version of code, inputs, or selection; transcription only when it says typo; otherwise unspecified with mismatch_cause=unclear.
